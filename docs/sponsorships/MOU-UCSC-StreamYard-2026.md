# Memorandum of Understanding (MOU): Foundational Supporter (In-Kind)

**Date of Agreement:** July 5, 2026  
**Parties:** Open Neuromorphic (ONM) and UC Santa Cruz (Represented by Jason Eshraghian)  
**Sponsorship Tier:** Foundational Supporter (In-Kind)

## 1. Purpose and Contribution Scope
This agreement formalizes the in-kind support provided by UC Santa Cruz (UCSC) to Open Neuromorphic. UCSC has generously agreed to cover the operational costs of ONM's live-streaming infrastructure.

*   **Contribution:** Direct payment of the StreamYard Core (annual) subscription.
*   **Term Covered:** July 5, 2026 – July 5, 2027
*   **Value:** $259.13 USD (Receipt #2797-8331, paid July 5, 2026)

## 2. Recognition and Acknowledgment
In accordance with the ONM Sponsorship Policy v1.0, UCSC is recognized as a **Foundational Supporter (In-Kind)**.

To acknowledge this contribution, ONM will provide the following recognition on the official ONM Supporters page:
*   Placement of the UC Santa Cruz logo and a link to the institution in the "Foundational Supporters" section.
*   The following dedicated acknowledgment text:
    > *"Special thanks to **UC Santa Cruz** and Jason Eshraghian for supporting Open Neuromorphic's live event infrastructure — their contribution keeps StreamYard running for our workshops, Student Talks, and community events."*
*   **Duration of Recognition:** This active recognition is scoped to a 1-year term (July 5, 2026 – July 5, 2027). Upon expiration of this term, the acknowledgment may be transitioned to a "historical contributions" section at ONM's discretion, but ongoing active placement is not guaranteed unless the sponsorship is renewed.

## 3. Editorial Independence & Policy Adherence
This agreement is governed entirely by the Open Neuromorphic Sponsorship Policy v1.0.
*   **No Editorial Influence:** In strict adherence to Principle 2.2 of the ONM Sponsorship Policy, ONM retains full and independent control over all content, event programming, and strategic direction. The Sponsor (UC Santa Cruz) shall have no editorial influence, approval rights, or veto power over ONM content, speakers, or activities as a result of this financial contribution.
*   **Clarification of Roles:** Jason Eshraghian is an active board/community member of ONM and exercises standard voting and community privileges through those independent channels. However, it is explicitly agreed that this sponsorship agreement confers no special or additional editorial influence to UCSC or Jason beyond his existing, standard community rights.
*   **Mutual Respect:** The sponsor agrees to interact with the community in a constructive and respectful manner, aligned with the ONM Code of Conduct.
